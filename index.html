B.U.G.H.A.O

A Proposed Student Governance Program for the University Student Senate

WMSU BUGHAO is a proposed centralized digital platform designed to strengthen student governance by providing a system to coordinate between the Main Campus and External Student Units (Local/USC), ensuring student leadership, providing accessible student resources, and maintaining transparency in university-wide student governance.

B
Bloated Concerns and Representation Student Voice

This letter provides students with a formal digital avenue to address, document, and prioritize student concerns, allowing community input in key decisions and ensuring that diverse perspectives are considered. All channels, automated or otherwise, are monitored for updates.

U
Uniform Campus and Ground Coordination Campus-Wide Sync

Addresses the structured communication and coordination channels between the Main Campus, External Student Units, the USC, and Local College Student Councils. It will help facilitate the dissemination of announcements, coordination of deliverables, and coordination of university-wide programs across campuses.

G
Grievance, Accomplishments & Reports Transparency

Provides a centralized space for monitoring accomplishments, program implementations, and student engagements from each Local College Council to the USC. It allows students to track their activities, ongoing initiatives, completed projects, and services delivered by their representatives.

H
Honoring Student Council Performance Recognition

This section focuses on monitoring performance, student congress records of conferences, positions, and program implementations, student engagements, and administration, Senate Committee records — properly tracking through the mechanism of financial reports and ensuring accountability.

A
Accessible Student Services and Information Student Support

Serves as a centralized system to constantly provide and disseminate information including guidance for requesting university documents, track academic requirements, deadlines, and updates on institutional matters. It will serve as a foundation for building an informed community for all students.

O
Overseeing Bodies and Public Records Accountability

Promotes transparency and accountability by providing an organized system to safely document the directives, executive orders, and resolutions of recognized university bodies. This includes regulations, legislative records, and protocols documenting the work of key governing organizations.

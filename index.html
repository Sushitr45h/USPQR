B.U.G.H.A.O. Blueprint

B — Bagong Administrasyon, Bagong Sistema
This reform agenda envisions a modern, organized and responsive University Student Council through institutional restructuring, constitutional reform and improved administrative systems. It aims to establish specialized departments that address students' diverse needs while strengthening accountability, representation and continuity in governance.
  • 1. Reorganization of University Student Council Structure (10 Departments)
  • 2. Constitutional Revision
  • 3. Restoring Constitutional Checks and Balances
    • (a) Commission on Audit (COA)
    • (b) Commission on Elections (COMELEC)
    • (c) Judicial Department
  • 1. Administrative Modernization through standardized procedures, digital records and clearer workflows.
  • 2. Institutional Continuity System to ensure proper documentation and turnover between outgoing and incoming administrations.
  • 3. Performance-Based Administration with defined responsibilities, deadlines and measurable departmental targets.
U — Unified Student Council Financial System
This financial framework establishes a unified financial management system for the USC and participating student councils through standardized accounting procedures, digital tools and transparent financial reporting. It aims to make budgets, official receipts, quantities, expenditures and remaining balances properly documented and accessible under established rules.
  • 1. One System, Shared Standards, Open Records
  • 2. Digital Financial Management Platform
  • 3. Unified Financial Reporting Standards
  • 4. ATM-Inspired Financial Mechanism .
  • 5. Regular Financial Disclosure
  • 6. Financial Management Training
G — Good Governance in Application
This accountability framework turns transparency and accountability into measurable practices through regular reporting, public disclosure, policy monitoring and performance evaluation. It introduces recognition for compliant councils and fair corrective measures for those that repeatedly fail to meet established requirements.
  • 1. Universal Transparency Reporting System
  • 2. Policy Implementation Monitoring
  • 3. Transparency and Disclosure Act
  • 4. Good Governance Awards
  • 5. Council Compliance and Performance Report
  • 6. Corrective Action and Accountability Mechanism
  • 7. WMSU Connect Website
Proposed Bills and Resolutions
(1) Monthly Transparency Reporting Resolution
(2) Transparency and Disclosure Act
(3) Student Council Accountability and Performance Act
(4) WMSU Connect Establishment Resolution
(5) Good Governance Awards and Recognition Resolution
(6) Policy Implementation Monitoring Resolution
H — Handa Makinig Bago Kumilos
This student-centered approach places students at the center of decision-making by ensuring that their concerns and recommendations guide the development of USC programs, projects, events and policies. Through accessible consultations and feedback systems, the USC will identify actual student needs before deciding on appropriate actions.
  • 1. Student Consultation Before Decisions
  • 2. Konsulta Bago Desisyon System
  • 3. Campus-Wide Student Surveys
  • 4. Student Feedback-to-Action Mechanism
  • 5. Minutes and Decision Disclosure
  • 6. Student Proposal System
A — Across Campuses, One Community
This campus integration initiative advances the One WMSU Initiative by strengthening coordination between the main campus and External Studies Units. It aims to improve communication, digitize administrative processes and ensure that ESU students participate meaningfully in consultations, meetings and university-wide decisions.
  • 1. Campus Connectivity System
  • 2. One WMSU Initiative
  • 3. Digitalized Inter-Campus Processes
  • 4. ESU Representation and Participation
  • 5. Inter-Campus Programs and Projects
  • 6. Unified Campus Information System
  • 7. ESU Needs Assessment
O — Open Door Policy
This student empowerment initiative makes the USC more accessible by opening opportunities for student participation, assistance, partnerships and development. It welcomes student proposals while connecting students to available programs, scholarships, training opportunities and official information through both physical offices and digital platforms.
  • 1. Opening Opportunities for Students
    • (a) Seminars and training workshops
    • (b) Information on available grants, scholarships and development programs
  • 2. Student-Initiated Programs and Projects providing channels for students and organizations to propose initiatives.
  • 3. Partnerships and External Linkages with government agencies, local government units, alumni, private institutions and community organizations.
  • 4. Community Engagement Programs including cleanup drives, outreach activities and environmental initiatives.
  • 5. Student Organization Collaboration involving recognized organizations in inclusive programs and shared projects.
  • 6. Open Access to USC Information through WMSU Connect, including transparency reports, accomplishment reports, announcements and student service information.
  • 7. Student Assistance and Referral System helping students reach the appropriate office or institution for support.

B.U.G.H.A.O

A Proposed Digital Governance Portal for the University Student Council

WMSU BUGHAO is a proposed centralized digital platform designed to strengthen student representation, improve coordination between the Main Campus and External Studies Units (ESUs), recognize student leadership, provide accessible student services, and institutionalize transparency and accountability within student governance.

The platform is guided by B.U.G.H.A.O., with each letter representing a core functionality of the system.

B - Boses
Student Concerns and Representation
Provides students with a formal digital avenue to submit concerns, complaints, processes, recommendations, and other matters directly to the University Student Council (USC). It aims to strengthen student participation and ensure that student concerns can be documented, referred to the appropriate offices, and monitored for updates.
U - Ugnayan
Campus and Council Coordination
Establishes a structured communication and coordination channel between the Main Campus, External Studies Units, the USC, and Local College Student Councils (CSCs). It aims to facilitate the proper routing of concerns, dissemination of information, and coordination of university-wide programs across campuses.
G - Gawa
Accomplishments, Announcements, and Implemented Projects or Programs
Provides a centralized space for publishing announcements, activities, projects, and accomplishments from Local CSCs to the USC. It allows students to stay informed about ongoing initiatives, completed projects, and services delivered by their student leaders.
H - Husay
Student Council Performance and Recognition
Introduces a recognition mechanism for outstanding student councils based on established performance indicators, such as program implementation, student engagement, transparency, responsiveness, and service delivery. It aims to encourage excellence, innovation, accountability, most especially to compliance with the requirements of financial report among local CSCs.
A - Akses
Accessible Student Services and Information
Serves as a centralized gateway to university services and essential student information, including guidance on requesting university documents such as Certificates of Registration (COR), identification cards, scholarship-related requirements, and other academic or administrative documents. It aims to make university services easier to navigate, particularly for students from External Studies Units.
O - Oportunidad / Bukas
Open Governance and Public Records
Promotes transparency and accountability by providing organized access to publicly discussable governance records, including USC and CSC financial reports, executive orders, Senate bills and resolutions, legislative records, and archives documenting the work of current and former student leaders.

B.U.G.H.A.O

B
Bagong Boses, Tugon, at Aksyon
Isang bukas at agarang sistema ng pagdinig sa mga hinaing at suhestiyon ng bawat estudyante. Titiyakin nating may konkretong aksyon ang konseho sa pamamagitan ng direktang konsultasyon, feedback channels, at regular na pakikipag-ugnayan sa pamunuan ng unibersidad upang tugunan ang mga pangunahing isyu tulad ng pasilidad, seguridad, at kapakanan ng mag-aaral.
U
Unified Student Council Financial System
Pagsasaayos at pagsasama-sama ng sistema ng pananalapi ng konseho para sa mas maayos at tapat na paggamit ng pondo. Titiyakin ang regular na paglalabas ng financial statements, transparent na liquidation ng bawat proyekto, at patas na alokasyon para sa mga inisyatibo na direktang kapaki-pakinabang sa buong komunidad ng WMSU.
G
Good Governance, Transparency, at Kilos
Pangungunahan ang isang pamumunong tapat, bukas, at may pananagutan sa bawat estudyante. Lahat ng resolusyon, polisiya, at desisyon ng konseho ay ilalatag sa publiko upang matiyak na ang kapangyarihan ay laging nasa kamay ng mga mag-aaral at para sa kanilang kapakanan.
H
Hinahangad na Pagbabago
Pagtataguyod ng mga makabuluhang reporma sa loob ng unibersidad na tumutugon sa tunay na pangangailangan ng bawat kolehiyo at External Studies Units (ESUs). Layunin nating gawing mas makatarungan, ligtas, at progresibo ang kapaligiran ng WMSU para sa lahat ng Crimson students.
A
Abot-Kamay na mga Dokumento at Serbisyong Mag-aaral
Gagawing mas mabilis, malinaw, at accessible ang pagproseso ng mga kailangang dokumento, requirements, at suportang pang-akademiko para sa bawat mag-aaral. Aalisin ang burukrasya at ilalapit ang serbisyo sa pamamagitan ng digital assistance at student-centered help desks.
O
Opisyal na Gabay at Responsableng Pamumuno
Pagbibigay ng malinaw na direksyon, institutional memory, at gabay sa mga lokal na konseho at student organizations. Sisiguraduhin nating ang pamumuno ay hindi natatapos sa pangako kundi nararamdaman sa bawat sulok ng ating unibersidad.

B.U.G.H.A.O

A Proposed Digital Governance Portal for the University Student Council

WMSU BUGHAO is a proposed centralized digital platform designed to strengthen student representation, improve coordination between the Main Campus and External Studies Units (ESUs), recognize student leadership, provide accessible student services, and institutionalize transparency and accountability within student governance.

The platform is guided by B.U.G.H.A.O., with each letter representing a core functionality of the system.

B - Boses
Student Concerns and Representation
Provides students with a formal digital avenue to submit concerns, complaints, processes, recommendations, and other matters directly to the University Student Council (USC). It aims to strengthen student participation and ensure that student concerns can be documented, referred to the appropriate offices, and monitored for updates.
U - Ugnayan
Campus and Council Coordination
Establishes a structured communication and coordination channel between the Main Campus, External Studies Units, the USC, and Local College Student Councils (CSCs). It aims to facilitate the proper routing of concerns, dissemination of information, and coordination of university-wide programs across campuses.
G - Gawa
Accomplishments, Announcements, and Implemented Projects or Programs
Provides a centralized space for publishing announcements, activities, projects, and accomplishments from Local CSCs to the USC. It allows students to stay informed about ongoing initiatives, completed projects, and services delivered by their student leaders.
H - Husay
Student Council Performance and Recognition
Introduces a recognition mechanism for outstanding student councils based on established performance indicators, such as program implementation, student engagement, transparency, responsiveness, and service delivery. It aims to encourage excellence, innovation, accountability, most especially to compliance with the requirements of financial report among local CSCs.
A - Akses
Accessible Student Services and Information
Serves as a centralized gateway to university services and essential student information, including guidance on requesting university documents such as Certificates of Registration (COR), identification cards, scholarship-related requirements, and other academic or administrative documents. It aims to make university services easier to navigate, particularly for students from External Studies Units.
O - Oportunidad / Bukas
Open Governance and Public Records
Promotes transparency and accountability by providing organized access to publicly discussable governance records, including USC and CSC financial reports, executive orders, Senate bills and resolutions, legislative records, and archives documenting the work of current and former student leaders.

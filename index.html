Project L.I.P.A.D

L — Leadership and Institutional Excellence
Strengthening the USC through institutional reform, effective leadership and student-centered decision-making.
  • (1) USC Organizational Restructuring — Establish ten specialized departments with clear responsibilities to improve the delivery of student services.
  • (2) Constitutional Revision — Review and improve the USC Constitution to address institutional gaps and strengthen governance.
  • (3) Restoring Checks and Balances — Strengthen the independence and accountability of oversight mechanisms within the student government.
  • (4) Konsulta Bago Desisyon — Conduct university-wide consultations and online surveys before deciding on major programs, projects and events.
  • (5) Transparent Decision-Making — Disclose approved minutes, resolutions and relevant decisions to keep students informed.
I — Inclusion, Incentives and Student Assistance
Expanding access to opportunities, practical assistance and recognition for deserving students.
  • (1) Scholarship Referral and Endorsement — Help eligible students access scholarship opportunities through referrals and assistance with application requirements.
  • (2) Student Entrepreneurship Support — Assist qualified student entrepreneurs in applying for available business spaces and university enterprise opportunities.
  • (3) USC Student Assistance Desk — Provide guidance on document processing, printing needs, application follow-ups and referrals to appropriate offices.
  • (4) Crimson Achievement Recognition — Recognize students who represent WMSU through competitions and notable achievements with appropriate incentives and tokens.
  • (5) Student ID Replacement Assistance — Help qualified students who cannot afford replacement fees obtain a new university identification card.
  • (6) Targeted Student Support — Develop transparent assistance programs for eligible working students, students with disabilities, dormitory residents and other students in need.
P — Programs, Participation and Opportunities
Delivering meaningful activities and development opportunities based on students' interests and needs.
  • (1) Student-Centered Events — Organize celebrations such as Valentine's Day, Ramadan Iftar and Women's Month based on student participation and feedback.
  • (2) Academic Year-End Activities — Conduct programs that celebrate student accomplishments and strengthen community engagement.
  • (3) Civil Service Review Initiative — Partner with College Student Councils to provide review opportunities for students preparing for civil service examinations.
  • (4) Leadership and Administrative Management Caravan — Bring leadership and governance training from the main campus to the External Studies Units.
  • (5) Open Seminar Opportunities — Open eligible external seminar and conference invitations to students through a transparent application and selection process.
  • (6) Crimson Student Support Draw — Provide selected student support packages through a transparent draw with published eligibility criteria and selection procedures.
A — Accountability and Financial Integrity
Establishing transparent financial practices, measurable performance standards and responsible student governance.
  • (1) Unified Student Council Financial System — Standardize financial reporting procedures across participating student councils to promote consistency and accountability.
  • (2) Digital Financial Management — Use digital tools to organize financial submissions and publish appropriate financial records for student access.
  • (3) ATM-Inspired Financial Mechanism — Coordinate with authorized university offices to assess the feasibility of dedicated accounts or financial cards for authorized transactions.
  • (4) Financial Management Training — Train USC, CSC and ESU finance officers in proper budgeting, documentation, liquidation and reporting.
  • (5) Council Compliance and Performance Report — Publish evidence-based assessments of financial reporting, accomplishment submissions and council performance.
  • (6) Officer Performance Evaluation — Assess officers and departments based on their responsibilities, deliverables and documented accomplishments.
  • (7) Good Governance Awards — Recognize councils that demonstrate consistent compliance, transparent reporting and effective project implementation.
  • (8) Corrective Action Mechanisms — Help noncompliant councils address verified deficiencies through clear deadlines, guidance and appropriate procedures.
D — Development, Digitalization and Campus Connectivity
Building a connected and inclusive Crimson Community through technology, coordination and shared development.
  • (1) WMSU Connect — Establish a centralized USC portal for minutes, resolutions, financial reports, accomplishment reports, the student handbook and other relevant public documents.
  • (2) One WMSU Initiative — Unite the main campus and External Studies Units through shared programs, consultations and collaborative projects.
  • (3) Inter-Campus Coordination — Serve as a communication and referral channel to help ESU councils raise concerns and follow up on requests.
  • (4) Digitalized Administrative Processes — Introduce appropriate online systems for submitting and tracking proposals, letters, reports and other documents.
  • (5) University-Wide Needs Assessment — Gather campus-specific concerns to guide student services, project planning and resource referrals.
  • (6) Inter-Campus Development Programs — Encourage collaboration in leadership, academics, sports, environmental initiatives and community engagement.
